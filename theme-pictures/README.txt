Put pictures for your themes in this folder.

Name each picture after its theme, then restart ArchipelagoVic or open Settings, Theme.
Use .png, .jpg, .webp, .gif or .avif. Tall, portrait pictures look best; they fill the bottom of the sidebar.
Either file name works for a theme:

  Archipelago            default.png  or  archipelago.png
  Midnight               midnight.png
  Parchment              sepia.png  or  parchment.png
  Forest                 forest.png
  Rosé                   rose.png
  High contrast          contrast.png  or  high-contrast.png
  Frieren                frieren.png
  Fullmetal Alchemist    fma.png  or  fullmetal-alchemist.png
  One Piece              onepiece.png  or  one-piece.png
  Naruto                 naruto.png
  Demon Slayer           demonslayer.png  or  demon-slayer.png
  Jujutsu Kaisen         jjk.png  or  jujutsu-kaisen.png
  Evangelion             eva.png  or  evangelion.png
  Sailor Moon            sailormoon.png  or  sailor-moon.png
  Cowboy Bebop           bebop.png  or  cowboy-bebop.png
  Attack on Titan        aot.png  or  attack-on-titan.png
  Spy × Family           spyfamily.png  or  spy-family.png

The pictures that come in this folder are original drawings by the author of ArchipelagoVic, shared with the project.
Replace any of them with your own by saving a picture with the same name here.
A picture chosen with "Choose picture..." in Settings takes priority over the one in this folder.
