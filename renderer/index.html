BizHawk games

One-click Play for games that run in the BizHawk emulator, like Pokémon Red and Blue. Play patches your ROM, starts EmuHawk with the Archipelago connector, starts the BizHawk Client and opens the room here.

Your ROMs stay where they are; the patched game is saved next to the patch file, as Archipelago does. Saving a game also sets its ROM and EmuHawk in host.yaml, so Archipelago's own tools use the same ones (your original host.yaml is kept as host.yaml.apvic-backup).

Worlds

      
Games that don't ship with Archipelago come as .apworld files. Installed ones live in .

    

    

    

    
ArchipelagoVic checks each file before installing: that it's a real APWorld, which game it is, and whether your Archipelago version is new enough. Archipelago programs that are already open need a restart to see a new world. Removing a world moves it to the Recycle Bin.

host.yaml

    
Defaults for hosting, generating and every game client. A backup is kept as host.yaml.bak each time you save.

    
    

    
Window

    

      
      
Your rooms stay connected and alerts keep coming. Click the tray icon to open it again; right-click it to quit. Turn this off to quit when you close the window.

    

    
Theme

    
Changes every colour in ArchipelagoVic, its room tabs and the mini window. Item colours keep their meaning in every theme: gold for key items, blue for useful, red for traps.

Links and pictures

    

      
      
Links from a room page join the game right here. Turn this off to hand links back to the official Launcher.

      
      
Icons come from SteamGridDB and Steam, covers and summaries from Steam and Wikipedia. Each is downloaded once and kept on this PC. Right-click any icon to use your own.

    

    
Icons for games that aren't on Steam

    
Steam games get their icon automatically. For the rest (Zelda, Pokémon, Kirby…) SteamGridDB has icons, but it needs a free API key from your SteamGridDB account. Get your key, then paste it here.

Reading the colors

    
Items are colored by how important they are

    
    
      
  • ★Hookshot
    Key item
    Unlocks new areas or abilities. Somebody is blocked until they get it, so these matter most.
    • 
      
  • ▲Heart Container
    Useful
    Makes life easier, but nobody is stuck without it.
    • 
      
  • ✖Ice Trap
    Trap
    Does something bad to whoever receives it.
    • 
      
  • ●20 Rupees
    Filler
    Currency, ammo and small bonuses. Nice to have.
    • 
    

    
Players have their own colored square

    
Each player keeps the same square color everywhere, so you can follow one person through the log. The color doesn't mean anything else. Your name carries a Your name tag.

    
Highlighted rows involve you

    
Rows with a teal edge are items you sent or received, hints about your items, or your own chat.

    
Hint status

    
Priority you want this item soon. Not needed you can skip it. Avoid it's a trap or harmful. Found it has already been picked up.

  




  

    
Alerts

    
Get a sound and a desktop notification while you're in your game. Notifications only appear when ArchipelagoVic isn't the window you're looking at.
