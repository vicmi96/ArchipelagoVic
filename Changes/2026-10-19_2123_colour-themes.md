# ArchipelagoVic 2.5.0: colour themes, including anime-inspired palettes

**Date:** 2026-10-19 21:23 (local time)
**Request:** "Can we add now a possibility to change the apps theme, not just to dark/light but actual themes that changes everything about colors? Let's also have themes associated with anime characters, like Frieren, Full metal alchemist, One piece, etc."

## What's new

**Settings → Theme** is a grid of theme cards. Each card shows a small preview of that theme's page, panel, text, accent button and highlight colour. Click one to switch.

- **The whole app follows the choice:** the main window, every room tab and the mini window switch together.
- **It's remembered.** In the browser-only client, the "Theme" button opens the same picker.

## What a theme changes

**Changed by every theme:**
- page background, panels and raised panels;
- lines and borders;
- all three text levels;
- the accent colour (buttons, links, selected tabs, progress bars, the "you" badge) and its soft tint;
- a second accent colour;
- for some themes, a subtle background glow or pattern.

**Kept on purpose:**
- **Item colours keep their meaning in every theme:** gold ★ key item, blue ▲ useful, red ✖ trap, grey ● filler. Each theme uses the light or dark version of these that matches it. This way a gold item never means something different in another theme.
- **Readability was checked for every theme with WCAG contrast ratios:**
  - main text: at least 7:1;
  - secondary text: at least 4.5:1;
  - accent text and links: at least 4.5:1;
  - text on accent buttons: at least 4.5:1;
  - item colours on panels: at least 3:1.

  All 17 pass. Frieren's sage accent was darkened slightly to get there.
